Notable Server Features

Outpost Alpha may be a new, small, community-run SMP. But you'll think its been around forever because of the way its profetionally (I cant remember how to spell that for some reason) run, has awesome custom made plugins/features, and an amazingly dedicated community of truely awesome people.

Cross-Play Ready

Java and Bedrock players share the same world, so it doesn't matter what platform you or your friends play on.

Custom Plugins

We strive to make as many quality of life and cosmetic improvements to the server as we can while still keeping the vanilla feel of the game you know and love through our custom plugins.

Actively Maintained

Outpost Alpha is privately owned and funded. If you'd like to chip in toward server costs, it's genuinely appreciated — but never required.
